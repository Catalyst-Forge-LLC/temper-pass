## Terms that pass here

- **temper pass / temper mill / skin-pass** — the product is the mill operation
- **load-bearing** — protocol term: an assumption whose opposite changes the recommendation
- **pass, called, auto** — the collection vocabulary
- **temper straw / purple / blue** — severity scale, not decoration
- **forcing function** — what the auto pass keeps when it still answers
- **tempered judgment** — the brand phrase for what the skills install; "wisdom" stays out of the copy (it claims more than a protocol can prove). Masthead subtitle: "Tempered judgment for AI agents."
